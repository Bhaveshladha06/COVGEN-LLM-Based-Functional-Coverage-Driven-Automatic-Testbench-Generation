Cover every opcode the ALU supports, from 0 through 7. For each opcode, cover
the operands at their minimum, maximum and mid-range values. Cover the carry
output being set and clear, and cover the zero flag being set and clear. Cross
the opcode against the zero flag so that every operation is seen producing both
a zero and a non-zero result where that is possible. Cover addition overflowing
into the carry, and subtraction borrowing.
