Cover a completed transfer on the input side, where in_valid and in_ready are
both high on the same clock edge, and likewise on the output side for out_valid
and out_ready. Cover the stall cases, which matter more than the transfers:
in_valid high while in_ready is low, and out_valid high while out_ready is low.
