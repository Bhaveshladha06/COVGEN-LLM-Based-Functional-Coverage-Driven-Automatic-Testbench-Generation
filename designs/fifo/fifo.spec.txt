Cover the FIFO becoming completely full and completely empty, and every
occupancy level in between. Cover a simultaneous read and write while the FIFO
is partially filled, so the occupancy stays unchanged. Cover a write attempted
while the FIFO is full and a read attempted while it is empty, confirming both
are rejected without corrupting the occupancy. Cover the cross of the write
enable against the read enable, and the cross of each enable against the full
and empty flags. Cover the data input at its minimum, maximum and mid-range
values, and cover the reset asserted while the FIFO holds data.
